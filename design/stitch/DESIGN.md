## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes executive-level academic rigor and technical governance with the warmth, tactile softness, and prestige of modern luxury hospitality. Tailored for enterprise specification workflows, audit trails, and collaborative engineering governance, the aesthetic balances high information density with serene, luminous whitespace.

The visual style is **Warm Minimalist Precision**:
- Uncluttered, structured layout geometry that handles intricate data grids, clause comparisons, and status indicators without visual noise.
- A delicate balance of cool deep teals and luminous golden ambers, projecting authority, security, and refined polish.
- Micro-glassmorphism applied selectively to contextual cards and floating governance rails, supported by crisp, whisper-thin borders and deliberate typographical hierarchy.
- A calm, focused mood designed to reduce cognitive fatigue during multi-hour technical audits and spec modeling sessions.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast deep teals and rich ambers on an airy, pearlescent base:

- **Primary (`#0B3C43`)**: Deep architectural teal. Represents structural integrity, authority, and definitive action. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation items, critical chips, and dominant callouts.
- **Secondary (`#8B7032`)**: Polished antique gold. Bestows warmth, verification, and accreditation. Used for governance status badges, verified certification markers, and secondary focal actions.
- **Tertiary (`#1EB4B6`)**: Vibrant aqua cyan. Highlights dynamic telemetry, real-time sync states, live AI assistance, and interactive focal points.
- **Neutral (`#111827`)**: Deep rich slate-charcoal. Provides WCAG AAA compliant text contrast against pure white and luminous canvas backgrounds.
- **Named Surfaces & Borders**:
  - `surface-canvas` (`#F6F9F9`): Soft, pearl-tinted background that eliminates glare while maintaining a clean, technical atmosphere.
  - `surface-tint-teal` (`#EFF6F7`) & `surface-tint-gold` (`#FCF9F1`): Contextual backgrounds for elevated panels, filter rails, and verified containers.
  - `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`) & `border-teal` (`#D1DEDE`): Restrained, low-contrast structural dividers that maintain dense alignment without visual weight.

## Typography

The type system utilizes **Inter** across all typographic applications, prioritizing clear legibility, geometric balance, and clean tabular alignment for complex specification documents.

- **Scale & Rhythm**: Compact baseline tracking paired with generous line-heights in body text guarantees extended reading comfort during auditing workflows.
- **Micro-labels & Status Indicators**: Uppercase tracking with medium/bold weights (`label-sm`, `label-md`) is applied to compliance tags, status flags, and table column heads.
- **Heading Character**: Headlines leverage tight negative letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to project architectural solidity and premium editorial confidence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt-derived fluid grid with high-density adaptations suited for technical governance:

- **Grid Alignment**: 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`1280px` max main content container; expanded full-width for multi-pane specification matrices).
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - *Desktop (≥1024px)*: 12 columns, `gutter-lg` (2rem), section `margin-lg` (3rem). Supports dual-pane document navigation and inspector panels.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1023px)*: 8 columns, `gutter` (1.25rem), `margin` (1.5rem). Sidebars collapse into floating overlay rails.
  - *Mobile (<768px)*: 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (0.75rem), `margin-sm` (1rem). Dense single-column stacks with sticky bottom action trays.
- **Rhythm**: Component padding prioritizes spatial efficiency (`space-sm` to `space-md` within tables and interactive rows) while sections breathe with `space-xl` and `space-2xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes ethereal lightness, crisp separation, and atmospheric depth over heavy dropshadows:

- **Base Surfaces**: The primary application canvas rests on `#F6F9F9`. Cards, audit sheets, and form surfaces sit at level 1 on pure `#FFFFFF` with ultra-fine borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Elevated Cards & Popovers**: Floating modals, dropdown menus, and active spec cards use soft, dual-tier ambient shadows:
  - *Ambient Shadow*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 60, 67, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 60, 67, 0.03)`.
  - *Highlight Glow*: Active and hovered governance cards gain a 1px border highlight (`#D1DEDE`) and subtle teal-tinted elevation.
- **Frosted Overlays**: Global headers and fixed utility drawers utilize micro-glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), allowing content to scroll beneath with translucent grace.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, refined curvature model:

- **Structural Containers & Cards**: Set to `rounded-md` (8px / `0.5rem`) and `rounded-lg` (12px / `0.75rem`) to convey structured technical governance without harsh right angles.
- **Interactive Triggers & Micro-Badges**: Action buttons, search bars, and status chips apply continuous organic pill radii (`9999px`), echoing the soft, approachable luxury aesthetic seen in floating pill navigations and prompt bars.
- **Inner Controls**: Checkboxes, toggle switches, and nested table tags maintain a tight `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius for high density and visual clarity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid deep teal (`#0B3C43`), white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.625rem 1.5rem`. On hover, subtle lift with a slight brightness boost (`#0E4D56`) and gentle cyan ambient glow.
- **Secondary (Accredited / Gold)**: Rich amber (`#8B7032`), white text, pill-shaped. Reserved for high-value governance operations, export verifications, and certification events.
- **Outlined / Ghost**: 1px border in `#D1DEDE`, charcoal text (`#111827`), hover background in `#EFF6F7`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges**: Pill-shaped with subtle tinted backgrounds and contrasting text:
  - *Active / Online*: `#EFF6F7` background, `#0B3C43` text, with a pulsing `#1EB4B6` indicator dot.
  - *Verified / Golden*: `#FCF9F1` background, `#8B7032` text, with a subtle `#E6CA85` hairline border.
- **Filter Chips**: Crisp white fill, `#E2E8F0` border, `12px` font size. Active state switches to primary deep teal fill with white typography.

### Input Fields & Search Trays
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `8px` corner radius, `0.625rem 1rem` padding.
- **Focus State**: `1px solid #1EB4B6` border with a 3px ring of `rgba(30, 180, 182, 0.15)`. No harsh dark focus rings.
- **Contextual Search**: Pill-shaped floating search bars with soft ambient teal drop-shadows and subtle magnifying glass iconography in `#52606D`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes**: 18px square with 4px border radius. Unchecked state features a 1.5px `#D1DEDE` border over white. Checked state fills with `#0B3C43` displaying a crisp white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Concentric rings with `#0B3C43` primary fill and an inner white core.

### Cards & Governance Containers
- **Standard Card**: Pure white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, 12px rounded corners, padding `1.25rem`. 
- **Featured / Verified Card**: Pure white background with an illuminated upper accent border (`2px solid #8B7032` or `#1EB4B6`), housing metadata tags, clause descriptions, and primary pill CTAs.

### Specification Tables & Data Grids
- **Header**: `#EFF6F7` with uppercase `label-sm` text in `#52606D`.
- **Row Styling**: Alternating hover highlights (`rgba(239, 246, 247, 0.5)`), separated by razor-thin `1px solid #F1F5F9` lines.
